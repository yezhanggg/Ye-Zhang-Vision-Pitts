# Product judge review — VisionPitts, Track 3

Reviewer role: senior Pittsburgh housing planner, Track 3 judge, 8 minutes per team. Reviewed Sat 2026-09-26 ~19:00–20:15 ET on the shipping repo.
Method: dev server on :5181 and `export/index.html` over `file://`, Playwright 1.63 / Chromium (swiftshader) at 1440×900, every claim below was read off the DOM or a capture in `docs/review/shots/` (my folder; `docs/screenshots/` untouched). Internet was on, so Photon and the Census geocoder answered.
Preset clicks read correctly with a 2.5 s settle; the one "stale headline" I saw at 300 ms was my harness, not the app.
Tree audited: commit `2b2a1f1` as served by the dev server. At 18:35 ET a layout refactor landed **uncommitted** in another session (floating, collapsible panels; a "Panels" menu; header chips Copy link / Sources & method / Reduce motion; step numbers removed; new `app/src/components/PanelFrame.tsx`). One smoke pass on that tree (`Z1-new-match-hazelwood.png`, `Z2-new-match-city.png`) shows identical content and numbers, so every finding below still applies; the two layout remarks in §3 are written against the new tree.

**What is good (one line):** the Match tract panel — answer card, six factor cards with the raw value, source, year and confidence chip, and a per-tract data-limits list — plus the Compare-scenarios flip map ("16 of 114 tracts change their best match") is the most honest, application-ready tract read I have seen in a hackathon tool, and it delivers the Track 3 brief (≥2 scenarios, adjustable normative weights, evidence vs values labeled).

## 1. First 60 seconds (landing → first click)

- Landing (`A1-landing.png`): a live 3D map, "Great decisions need vision. We give you one.", four tiles. Nothing on this page says *which housing type, for whom (≤50% AMI renters), without displacement, in Pittsburgh*. The primary tile reads "Browse the census, then match housing types to places" — the census browser is billed first, the Track 3 tool second.
- First click "Open VisionPitts" → globe flight with a title card; the Skip button is on screen until **13.4 s** (`A2b-intro-6s.png`) → lands on **Explore** (`A3-explore-after-intro.png`), a census browser with "Browse the census, one shape at a time." The matchmaker is behind the header pill "Analysis" or a small "Analysis →" button in the right panel.
- The first rewarding click (a tract answer) is four actions away: Open → Skip → Analysis → a demo chip. A judge on an 8-minute clock spends 2 of them on the intro and the census browser. Once on Match with Hazelwood (`E1-match-hazelwood.png`: "Small apartment · 65 / 100 match … Likely pick. Stays #1 in 6 of 10") the product is immediately legible — that screen should be the landing's first door.
- Where I got lost: on Explore I tried to find "the score" in the Data panel (37 ACS variables, none scored). The tagline never says "Analysis is the matchmaker".

## 3. Usability for a non-technical planner

- **Labels**: strong plain language ("Higher than 91% of city tracts", "Some · About 1/6 of the decision", "Solid pick. Stays #1 in 9 of 10 small changes"); factor (i) tips explain QCT/DDA/OZ/CDBG. Weak: "Which type wins" vs "Best match" needs one line of help; "Need × market change (2016→2021)" is analyst-speak — call it "Watch list".
- **Colors**: score ramp (cream → maroon) and factor ramp (greens) are correctly distinct. Typology palette is Okabe-Ito (good), but on the city map yellow (senior) and pink (townhome) dominate while small apartment is dark red, which reads as "alarm" for the deep-affordability product; consider swapping small apartment to the blue that is currently unused.
- **Reading order**: the left panel (Find a place → What matters most → Fine-tune → Color the map by → Save & compare) and the right panel (answer → ranking → pressure → about → data → limits) are right; the refactor dropped the 1–5 step numbers, which were helping first-time users — consider keeping them. **Map framing is wrong at 1440×900**: with no tract selected the city's east end projects at x≈1270 and Squirrel Hill at x≈1009, both under the 440-px right panel that starts at x≈997 (`Z2-new-match-city.png`; on the committed tree it was worse, x≈1456/1191, `E3b-city-which-type-wins.png`) — Squirrel Hill, Point Breeze, Homewood, Regent Square are hidden until you drag. **M** `MatchView.tsx`: the new `padding` reacts to hidden panels but nothing fits the city; call `fitBounds(cityBounds, padding)` on entering Match with no tract.
- **Behind "How this is calculated" that should be in front**: (1) "scored on N of 6 factors" and (2) the low-income renter count ("590 renter households ≤50% AMI") belong on the answer card — they are the two facts a CDC director reads first; (3) the fit direction ("Lower is better for Small apartment") is already in front — good.
- **In front that should go behind**: B-table ids in the Data rows; the Dewey caveat repeated three times on one card (rent line, its (i), Data limits); the 3D-buildings legend line; the technical Dirichlet note is already behind — keep it there.
- **Copy nits**: "about the same than"; "There is no factor data" at zero weights; "Fewer than 25 households" for a 6,035-person campus; duplicate Photon rows; scenario names that no longer match their weights.

## Five fixes with the biggest judge impact by Sunday evening

1. **S — Land on the answer.** Primary tile → `#m=match&t=42003562300`, tile text states the question (≤50% AMI renters, no displacement, five types); intro ≤4 s. `LandingPage.tsx`, `ExploreView.tsx`, `MapView.tsx`. Turns the first 60 s from "a census browser with a globe" into "Hazelwood: Small apartment 65/100, likely pick".
2. **S — Fix the why-sentence direction.** `lib/derived.ts` (`marginDrivers`, `describeFactor`, `reasons`): never cite a negatively-weighted factor at a high percentile as a reason. Today North Shore gets townhomes "because a lot of flood-prone land" — a Pittsburgh judge will catch it in seconds.
3. **M — Ties and single-factor degeneracy.** `derived.ts` + `TypologyRankList.tsx`/`StabilityBadge`: detect ties, say "tie", hide "Solid pick"; also the zero-weights message. Any judge who drags one slider hits this.
4. **M — Senior housing wins 40 of 114.** Either apply the `assumptions.md` §2 option (senior market 0.15, flood −0.5) in `config/scoring.json` + `app/src/data/scoring.json` and re-run the parity fixture and tests, or put a one-line reason on the card and rename the type "Subsidized senior/supportive". Add "scored on N of 6 factors" and the ≤50%-AMI renter count to `AnswerCard` (`MatchView.tsx`); grey zero-need tracts like South Shore 42003980700.
5. **S/M — Framing and missing-data honesty.** Fit the city bounds with the live panel padding on Match entry (`MatchView.tsx`, `MapView.tsx flyToBounds`); drop null-side rows from `factorDeltas`/`compareTakeaway`; fix "Pick two places" for an unranked B; promote "Watch list" to a segmented button in `MetricPicker.tsx`; 3D-buildings legend only for demo tracts (`Legend.tsx`). Each is under an hour; together they remove every "wait, that's wrong" moment I hit in 75 minutes of use.
